# LinkedIn Post Performance Analyzer

A small tool that answers one question: **which of your LinkedIn posts actually reach people — and which format works best?**

## What it does

1. **Summary dashboard** — total posts, total impressions, average impressions, and your best-performing post.
2. **Plain-language insight** — reads your numbers and tells you which format wins and by how much (e.g. "Image posts reach about 120% more people than text + external link posts").
3. **Impressions chart** — every post as a bar, so the best and worst posts are visible at a glance. The best post is highlighted.
4. **Format comparison table** — average impressions and average engagement rate grouped by format: Image in post, Image + link in comment, Text only, Text + external link, Video.
5. **Add your own posts** — title, format, impressions, likes and comments. Your posts are saved in your own browser (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
6. **Delete and reset** — remove any post, or reset back to the sample data.

## Why format matters

LinkedIn shows text-only posts with an external link to the fewest people. An image in the post — with the link in the first comment instead — usually reaches far more people. This tool makes that difference visible in your own numbers instead of a guess.

## The sample data

The six posts included are **demo data only** — delete them and add your real numbers from LinkedIn Analytics (Profile → Analytics → Content) to get your own insight.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any browser — no build step, no server needed.

## Tech

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, no libraries.
